Jonah

2026  ·  23 pages  ·  Written and illustrated by jordankasky

A picture book retelling the biblical story of the Prophet Jonah, who is called by God to travel to the city of Nineveh and deliver a message to its people. Instead, Jonah attempts to flee from his calling, setting off a remarkable journey across the sea.

Through lively, hand-drawn illustrations, the story follows Jonah's journey from his attempt to escape God's command to the storm at sea, his encounter with the great fish, and his eventual arrival in Nineveh. A timeless story about faith, obedience, mercy, and second chances, suitable for young readers and families to enjoy together.

Inside the book
