Why We Kicked Out Spoiled Alice From Wonderland

Published 2025  ·  15 pages  ·  Written and illustrated by jordankasky

A picture book about a troublesome girl who stumbles into Wonderland and turns every curious encounter into chaos and confusion. Through lively, hand-drawn illustrations and playful prose, Why We Kicked Out Spoiled Alice invites young readers to laugh, cringe, and wonder whether Alice will ever learn her manners before the people of Wonderland finally lose their patience.

Each spread pairs a dreamlike illustration with a short verse, making it equally beautiful on a bookshelf and a joy to read aloud. Suitable for ages 3–8.
