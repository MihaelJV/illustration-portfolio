He Who Feeds the Birds

2026  ·  10 pages  ·  Written and illustrated by jordankasky

A fantasy comic about an angel who battles spirits while searching for a way back home. After losing his wing, he finds himself stranded and unable to return to the place he once belonged.

With nowhere else to turn, the angel seeks an answer from He Who Feeds the Birds. What he discovers changes his understanding of what he has lost, and ultimately gives him the means to fly again. A story about faith, perseverance, loss, and finding a way forward when the path home seems impossible.
